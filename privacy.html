SMS Returns – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026 · Applies to the PC (Steam) version of SMS Returns

This policy explains what information SMS Returns ("the Game") handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version: the Game does not collect, store or sell any personal data on servers run by us. Everything below describes information that stays on your computer, or that passes through Steam, Epic Online Services (voice chat only) or Discord under their own privacy policies.

1. Who we are

SMS Returns is developed and published by Dark Pathogen Studios ("we", "us"). You can reach us about privacy at marcsteene@yahoo.co.uk or through the SMS Returns Community Hub discussions on Steam.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not run any servers of our own. There are no accounts for you to create or sign in to, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising in the Game.
  • The Game does not include a crash reporter and does not send crash data to us.
  • We never see your Steam or Discord credentials, email address, payment details or IP address. The voice chat service described in section 5 is operated by Epic Games, which does receive your IP address when the Game connects to it.

3. Information stored on your computer

Save games, custom maps, playlists, character profiles, settings and progress are stored locally in your user profile folder. If Steam Cloud is enabled for the Game, Steam may synchronise some of these files between your own devices under the Steam Privacy Policy. You can delete this data at any time by deleting the Game's save folder or uninstalling the Game.

4. Steam

The Game runs on Steam and uses Steam features provided by Valve Corporation. Through Steam, the Game handles:

  • Your Steam display name, shown to other players in multiplayer lobbies and matches.
  • Achievements and statistics, stored by Steam against your Steam account.
  • Multiplayer sessions. When you host or join a game, Steam relays the lobby name typed by the host, player names, text chat messages, and gameplay state between the players in that session. Hosts can kick or ban players for the duration of a session; bans are kept only in memory and are discarded when the session ends.
  • Voice chat is no longer carried by Steam. It is described in section 5.
  • Steam Workshop. If you publish a custom map, its name, description, preview image and map file are uploaded to Steam Workshop under your Steam account and are publicly visible. You can edit or delete Workshop items at any time from Steam.
  • Steam Rich Presence. While the "Activity Status" option is on (Settings → General → Gameplay; it is on by default), the Game tells Steam what you are doing so that your Steam friends can see it in their friends list: which part of the Game you are in or the level you are playing, whether you are playing solo or online, the selected game mode preset, and the lobby join code only when you are in a lobby the host has set to Public. Lobby names and other players' names are never included. Turning Activity Status off clears this immediately.

Valve processes this information under the Steam Privacy Policy and the Steam Subscriber Agreement.

5. Voice chat (Epic Online Services)

From version 0.9.6, in-game voice chat is provided through Epic Online Services, a service operated by Epic Games, Inc. The Game uses it for voice chat only. You do not create or sign in to an Epic Games account, and no Epic launcher or overlay is involved.

  • Background sign-in. The Game does not contact Epic Online Services until you need voice chat: the first time you host or join an online session after starting the Game, or when you start the microphone test in Settings. If you only play offline, the Game never signs in. When it does, it signs in in the background using a ticket issued by Steam that proves which Steam account is playing. Epic creates and stores an anonymous player ID for SMS Returns that is linked to your Steam account ID, together with the time you were last seen, and may store your Steam display name. Once you have gone online, this happens whether or not you go on to speak.
  • Connection information. As with any online service your computer connects to, Epic receives your IP address and basic technical information about your device and connection.
  • Voice rooms. When you host or join an online session, the Game places you in a private voice room for that session. Your Steam account ID is shared with the other members of that room so the Game can match each voice to the right player (for speaking indicators, mute and per-player volume). A host who kicks or bans you also removes you from the voice room.
  • Your voice. The "Voice Chat" option (Settings → General) is set to Open Mic by default, which sends your microphone audio while you are in an online session. You can change it to Push To Talk, which sends audio only while you hold the key, or to Off, which sends nothing and mutes everyone else. Voice audio is relayed through Epic's servers to the other players in your session. While you are alone in a session the voice service does not capture your microphone at all. The Game does not record voice chat, and we never receive it.
  • Microphone test. The optional microphone test in Settings sends your microphone audio to Epic's servers, which send it straight back to you so you can hear how you sound. Nobody else receives it, and it stops by itself after 90 seconds.

Epic Games, Inc. is based in the United States and processes this information under the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

6. Discord Rich Presence

If the Discord desktop app is running on your computer, the Game can show what you are doing on your Discord profile ("Rich Presence"). The Game sends only the following to your local Discord app:

  • Which part of the Game you are in (main menu, character editor, level editor, lobby, or the name of the level you are playing).
  • Whether you are playing solo, in local co-op, or online, the selected game mode preset, and the number of players in your session.
  • The lobby join code, only when you are in a lobby that the host has set to Public.

The Game never asks you to log in to Discord, does not read your Discord account, friends, messages or voice, and does not send any other information to Discord. How Discord displays and stores presence information is governed by the Discord Privacy Policy. You can turn Rich Presence off at any time with the "Activity Status" option (Settings → General → Gameplay), which also stops the Steam Rich Presence described in section 4, or by closing Discord; the Game then sends nothing. This page can also be opened from the same settings page through the "Privacy Policy" button.

7. Other players

Anything you type in in-game chat, say over voice chat, or include in a custom map that you share is visible to the other players in that session or to anyone who downloads the map. Please do not include personal information in chat, map names, descriptions or map content.

8. Children

SMS Returns is a horror game and is not directed at children. Steam requires account holders to be at least 13 years old, and local age ratings may set a higher minimum. We do not knowingly handle personal information from children.

9. Your rights

We hold no personal data on systems of our own. Local data is under your own control as described in section 3, and Steam, Epic Games and Discord provide their own tools for the data they hold. The one record kept on our behalf is the anonymous voice chat player ID described in section 5, which Epic stores linked to your Steam account ID. You can ask us for a copy of that record or to have it deleted: contact us with your Steam account ID and we will make the request through Epic's developer tools, which can take a few weeks to complete. Deleting it does not affect your Steam account or your saved games, and a new ID is created the next time you start the Game. If you believe we have any other personal data of yours, or you have any privacy question, contact us at marcsteene@yahoo.co.uk and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future update changes what the Game handles (for example, new online features), we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced on the Game's Steam Community Hub.
